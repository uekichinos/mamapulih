## Privacy Notice

**[Business Name]** ("we", "us") operates a confinement meal booking and delivery service. This notice explains what personal data we collect through our booking system, why, and how we protect it.

### What we collect

- **Contact details:** name, email address, phone number.
- **Delivery address** and the zone it falls in.
- **Booking details:** expected delivery date, birth type, package and add-ons chosen, delivery dates.
- **Dietary information:** allergies and food exclusions you tell us about, so we can prepare your meals safely.
- **Payment records:** amounts paid, payment dates, and the method used (we do not store full card or bank account numbers — manual bank transfers are recorded by reference number only).
- **Account credentials:** your password is stored encrypted and is never visible to our staff.

### Why we collect it

- To process and deliver your confinement meal booking.
- To contact you about your booking (confirmations, payment reminders, delivery schedule, pauses, and any changes).
- To accommodate your dietary needs safely.
- To issue receipts and manage payments and refunds.

### Who can see it

Access is limited by role: kitchen staff see only what's needed to prepare and deliver your meals (your name, address, and dietary needs) — never your payment details. Only the business owner/admin account can see your full booking and payment history.
